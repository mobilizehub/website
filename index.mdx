title: "Introduction"
---

MobilizeHub is an open-source plugin that extends Payload CMS with powerful advocacy and engagement tools:

- **Contact Management** - Build and segment your supporter database with tags and opt-in tracking
- **Email Broadcasts** - Create and send targeted email campaigns with audience segmentation
- **Petitions** - Launch petition campaigns with signature tracking and automated contact management
- **Letters** - Launch letter campaigns with custom targets and allow supporters to customise their emails
- **Forms & Submissions** - Collect and manage user responses with customizable forms
- **Engagement Tracking** - Monitor email delivery, opens, clicks, and bounces via webhooks
- **Secure Unsubscribe** - HMAC-SHA256 signed tokens for secure email unsubscribe handling
